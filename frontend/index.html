Supporting UN Sustainable Development Goals

AgroSustain directly advances 5 key United Nations SDGs to build climate-resilient, food-secure, and ecologically harmonious agricultural communities.

2

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

Maximizes crop yields, reduces harvest losses, and enhances staple food security through precision AI nutrient advice.

6

SDG 6: Clean Water

Prevents aquifer over-extraction and protects freshwater watersheds by optimizing precision drip and sprinkler runtimes.

12

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

Reduces chemical fertilizer and pesticide overuse through targeted bio-organic inputs and crop residue recycling.

13

SDG 13: Climate Action

Builds climate resilience against droughts and shifting precipitation while lowering agriculture's carbon footprint.

15

SDG 15: Life on Land

Protects soil microbial health, halts land degradation, and promotes biodiversity with cover crops and IPM.
